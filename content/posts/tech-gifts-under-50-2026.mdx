## Conclusion

The wireless charger and the light bar are the two picks here most likely to become something the recipient uses every single day without thinking about it — which, for a gift, is a better outcome than something flashier that gets used once. Match the gift to whether they're building out a full desk setup (mat, light bar) or just need better everyday essentials (charger, cable, power bank).
