Qi2 is the reason 2026 is a genuinely good year to upgrade your wireless charger. Magnetic alignment means your phone locks into the correct charging position every time — no more nudging a phone around a pad hoping it catches the coil. Here's what's actually worth putting on a desk.

- **Best Budget:** Anker MagGo Qi2 Pad
- **Best Premium Desk Station:** Belkin BoostCharge Pro 3-in-1
- **Best Foldable/Travel:** Spigen ArcField Qi2.2 25W

## Conclusion

For most desks, the Anker MagGo Pad at $16 is genuinely all you need — there's very little functional gap between it and pricier single-device pads. Upgrade to the Belkin 3-in-1 specifically if your desk regularly needs to charge a watch and earbuds alongside your phone, and consider the ESR CryoBoost only if you've noticed your current charger throttling down mid-charge.
