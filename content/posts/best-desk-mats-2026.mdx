A desk mat does more visual work per square inch than almost anything else on a desk — it's the surface everything else sits on top of. Material matters more here than in most desk-setup decisions, because it determines both how the mat looks in a year and how it looks today.

- **Best Premium Leather:** Orbitkey Desk Mat

- **Best Budget:** K KNODEL Dual-Sided PU Leather
