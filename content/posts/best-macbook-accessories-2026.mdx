A MacBook desk setup goes wrong in one of two directions: either you own nothing and live with two ports and a laptop balanced on a stack of books, or you own eleven dongles and your desk looks like an electronics recycling bin. This is the shortlist that avoids both — one pick per category, chosen specifically for MacBook compatibility and desk aesthetics, not just spec-sheet port counts.

## Quick Picks

- **Hub:** Satechi USB-C Pro Hub
- **Stand:** Rain Design mStand
- **Charger:** Belkin BoostCharge Pro 3-in-1
- **Desk Mat:** Orbitkey Desk Mat

## Conclusion

None of these four are essential in the sense that your MacBook won't work without them — but each solves a specific, real desk problem: not enough ports, screen too low, cables everywhere, phone charging chaos. Start with whichever problem is actually bothering you daily, not with the full list at once.
