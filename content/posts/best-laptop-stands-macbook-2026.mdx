A laptop sitting flat on a desk puts your screen well below eye level, which is where most "why does my neck hurt by 4pm" desk problems actually start. A stand fixes the ergonomics — the question below is which one, since stability, portability, and aesthetics pull in genuinely different directions.

## Quick Picks

- **Best Overall:** Rain Design mStand
- **Best Style-Forward Budget Pick:** Twelve South Curve SE
- **Best True Budget:** BESIGN LS03
- **Best Premium/Aesthetic:** Handcrafted Walnut Stand (Oakywood or Grovemade)

## Conclusion

The Rain Design mStand is the safe, proven pick if you want to buy once and be done. Go with the Twelve South Curve SE if the mStand's price feels like more than you want to spend but you still care about desk aesthetics, the BESIGN LS03 if you're genuinely budget-constrained, and the walnut stand only if you're already building a warm-wood desk aesthetic where it'll actually match.
