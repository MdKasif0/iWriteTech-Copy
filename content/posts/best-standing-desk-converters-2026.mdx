A standing desk converter sits on top of your existing desk and lifts your monitor, keyboard, and work surface to standing height — it's the answer for anyone who likes their current desk, doesn't have room for a full sit-stand desk, or isn't ready for that level of commitment yet.

## Conclusion

For most home offices, the Vivo K-Series is the right starting point — genuine sit-stand functionality without a $400+ commitment before you know if you'll actually use it daily. Step up to the VariDesk Pro Plus 36 once you're certain you want the category permanently and have the desk depth for it, and go with the CHANGEdesk Mini specifically if desk space is the binding constraint.
