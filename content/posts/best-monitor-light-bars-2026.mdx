A monitor light bar solves a specific problem a desk lamp can't: a lamp beside your monitor reflects straight off the screen, raising ambient glare exactly where you're trying to focus. A light bar mounted on top directs light down onto the desk instead — the geometry is the whole trick, not just the light source.

## Conclusion

The Quntis Pro+ is the right starting point for most people — it's genuinely good, not just 'good for the price.' Upgrade to the BenQ ScreenBar Halo 2 specifically if you've already tried a budget light bar and want the noticeably better glare elimination and hands-free motion sensing. The Xiaomi is the pick if your desk's visual design is the priority and the lighting performance just needs to be solidly good, not best-in-class.
